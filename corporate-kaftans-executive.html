Corporate Kaftans: Redefining Office Wear for the Nigerian Executive

Embracing minimal styling, deep conservative colors, and structured shoulders in boardrooms.

For decades, the standard uniform for the corporate executive was the dark Western suit. However, in Nigeria and across West Africa, a new trend has emerged. The corporate kaftan is redefining office wear, offering a professional, culturally relevant alternative that is suited for both boardroom meetings and corporate events.

A corporate kaftan is different from a casual one. It is characterized by structured shoulders, clean lines, and a minimalist design. The colors are kept conservative, with deep navy, charcoal grey, black, and dark brown being the preferred choices. Embroidery is minimal, often limited to subtle detailing along the placket or collar.

To style a corporate kaftan, pair it with well tailored trousers in the same fabric. The fit should be slim but comfortable, avoiding excess fabric that looks untidy. Wear it with polished leather Oxford shoes or high quality loafers. A classic leather watch and a neat haircut complete the look. By embracing the corporate kaftan, modern executives are proving that professionalism and cultural heritage can coexist beautifully.
