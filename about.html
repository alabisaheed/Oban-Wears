Our Story

About Oban

Bridging the gap between heritage and modern elegance, crafted in the heart of Lagos.

The Philosophy

Affordable Luxury

Oban Wears was founded on a simple conviction: that high quality, precise tailoring should not be a financial burden. We believe in empowering the modern gentleman with garments that exude confidence, sophistication, and cultural pride.

By blending avant garde concepts with timeless elegance, we architect urban men's fashion that defines identity and feels premium without compromise.

The Craft

West African Heritage

Based in Lagos, we specialize in crafting high quality Kaftans, Agbadas, and contemporary corporate apparel. We take pride in honoring traditional West African styles, adding modern twists that make them relevant for contemporary events, weddings, and formal occasions.

Each garment is meticulously cut in small runs, finished by skilled hands, and designed using expressive fabrics to ensure they speak softly and are always remembered.

The Story Behind Oban Wears

Who Founded Oban Wears?

Oban Wears was founded by Oba AbdulAzeez, a passionate tailor and entrepreneur whose journey began with a simple sewing machine and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

As a first generation tailor, AbdulAzeez did not inherit a fashion empire. He built one stitch at a time, learning, refining his craft, and dedicating years to mastering the art of bespoke tailoring. What started as a skill has evolved into a brand trusted by men who appreciate quality, precision, and timeless style.

Today, he leads Oban Wears with one vision: to create garments that do not just fit the body, but elevate the man wearing them.

Our Mission

Why Was Oban Wears Created?

Oban Wears was born from the belief that every man deserves to look exceptional without compromising on quality.

Too often, men had to choose between overpriced luxury and poorly made alternatives. We saw the gap and decided to bridge it by creating garments that combine premium craftsmanship, refined aesthetics, and lasting value.

Our goal has never been to simply make clothes. We exist to help men walk into every room with confidence, knowing they are dressed in something intentionally crafted for them.

The Inspiration

What Problem in Nigerian Fashion Inspired the Brand?

Nigeria is blessed with incredible creativity, yet many men still struggle to find tailors who consistently deliver excellent finishing, accurate fitting, and attention to detail.

Missed delivery dates, inconsistent quality, poor finishing, and garments that fail to match the client expectations have become common experiences.

Oban Wears was created to challenge that standard.

Every piece we produce reflects our commitment to precision, consistency, and craftsmanship. We believe that premium tailoring should be dependable, and every client should receive clothing that feels as remarkable as it looks.

Our Philosophy

What Philosophy Guides Our Tailoring?

Our philosophy is simple: Craftsmanship over shortcuts. Quality over compromise. Elegance that never goes out of style.

Every garment is guided by three non negotiable standards: precision in every measurement, attention to detail in every stitch, and neat finishing in every piece we deliver.

We do not chase trends. We create timeless masculine pieces that remain relevant long after the moment has passed.

At Oban Wears, tailoring is more than a service, it is an art. Every fabric is carefully selected, every detail thoughtfully considered, and every garment intentionally crafted to reflect the confidence, class, and excellence of the man wearing it.

Because when you wear Oban Wears, you are not just putting on clothes. You are wearing craftsmanship, confidence, and a standard that speaks before you do.

Our Why

Bridging the Gap

Oban Wears was founded on the belief that every deserving man should have access to quality clothing without compromising.

Yet, we have seen how true quality often comes at a cost that pushes many away.

We chose to challenge that. By combining top notch craftsmanship, refined design, and thoughtful pricing, Oban Wears bridges the gap between aspiration and reality, making it possible for more men to look sharp, feel confident, and present themselves with class, without unnecessary financial strain.
